## What I research

My research asks one question. Can AI agents plan, predict and act safely in high-stakes operations? I work on world models, JEPA, reinforcement learning and ways to test how reliable autonomous agents really are. The aim is an agent you can govern before it acts.

Hercyna comes from that work. A memory that drops the wrong record can make an agent act wrong. I wanted a neutral way to measure it.

## Why I built Think and Reason

Answers are cheap and instant now. I use AI tools every day. That is why I worry about what cheap answers do to thinking.

Think and Reason is a place to practise first-principles thinking. You write your own first thought before you look anything up, with people doing the same. Think first, then use any tool.

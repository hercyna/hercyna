## The myth

In Greek myth, Hercyna is the nymph of a river gorge. Two springs flow there side by side. Lethe is the spring of forgetting. Mnemosyne is the spring of memory.

Seekers who came to the oracle bathed in the Hercyna first. They drank from Lethe to clear away the noise. Then they drank from Mnemosyne to keep only what mattered.

## Why the name fits

An agent's memory does the same work. It forgets most of a long history and keeps a small part. The bench asks one question about that boundary. Did the memory keep what the decision needed?

The name points at the boundary itself. It blames no memory product. It has seven letters and is easy to say: her-KY-nuh or HER-si-nuh.

The mark shows the two springs. The left stroke breaks into dots, for what is forgotten. The right stroke stays whole, for what is kept. A thin arch joins them, for the gorge.
